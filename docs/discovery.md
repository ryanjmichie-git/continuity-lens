- Schedule the later usability session at the same time.

## Fifteen-minute guide

Do not show Continuity Lens during discovery.

1. Tell me about the last clip or transition that failed continuity review.
2. How did you discover it, and what did you do next?
3. How much time or generation budget did it cost?
4. Which failures matter most: subject, motion, geometry, lighting, or editing continuity?
5. What is your current workaround?
6. What evidence would you need before trusting automated triage?
7. How often does this happen, and when is it severe enough to change your workflow?
