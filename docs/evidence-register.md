# Evidence register

| Evidence | Level | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen DAVIS 60/30 benchmark | **EMPIRICAL — model evaluation** | Performance on documented within-video corruptions, latency, and grouped uncertainty | Creator demand, real generative-video quality, or universal world-model capability |
| Six generated app examples | **EMPIRICAL — system behavior** | Reproducible UI execution and qualitative failure inspection | Statistical quality claims or natural-video validity |
| Twelve controlled geometry cases | **EMPIRICAL — synthetic diagnostic** | Directional stress-test behavior and concrete failure modes | Headline performance or real/generative-video validity |
| Expert cognitive walkthrough | **EXPERT REVIEW — simulated use** | Likely comprehension and interaction risks; bounded design changes | Human task success, observed timing, satisfaction, or adoption |
| Three archetype assumption cards | **HYPOTHESIS — archetype-based** | Candidate jobs, risks, and evidence needs worth testing | Interview findings, prevalence, budgets, willingness to pay, or validated demand |
| Human problem interviews | **ABSENT** | Nothing yet | No discovery claim may be made |
| Human usability sessions | **ABSENT** | Nothing yet | No participant completion or comprehension claim may be made |

## Claim policy

Every public claim should name its evidence level. “The cheap ensemble outperformed V-JEPA on the
frozen DAVIS corruption benchmark” is supported. “Creators need this tool” and “users understood
the score” are not supported. Simulated archetypes never receive participant IDs, quotations, or
observed completion times.
