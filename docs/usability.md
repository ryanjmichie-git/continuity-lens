# Usability evidence

## Status

**Pending human sessions.** This document is a protocol, not evidence that sessions occurred.

## Ten-minute task

For each of three participants:

1. Upload one known-good and one known-bad transition.
2. Identify the frames the interface considers the boundary.
3. Explain the overall risk and at least one component signal.
4. Decide whether to accept, edit, or regenerate the transition.
5. Explain one reason the tool could be wrong.

Capture task completion, time, confusion points, requested evidence, and the participant’s interpretation in anonymized form.

## Acceptance criterion

- All three complete the workflow without facilitator takeover.
- At least two correctly explain that the score is benchmark-calibrated evidence rather than physical truth.
- Iterate once on observed friction; do not tune the model or held-out protocol from usability feedback.
